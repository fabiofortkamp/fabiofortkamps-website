# Fabio Fortkamp's Website

Fabio's personal website: his home on the internet, showing who he is, the
work he has done and what he writes about.

## Language

**Project**:
A body of work Fabio did, either software (an app, a tool, his dotfiles, this
website, a contribution to someone else's code) or research (his PhD, his DTU
postdoc). A **Project** can have a **Parent Project** and any number of
**Related Projects**. Exercises from a book or course, and forks without a
**Role** worth naming, are not **Projects**.
_Avoid_: Work, portfolio item, case study

**Parent Project**:
The **Project** another **Project** was built as part of, for example My DTU
Postdoc for packgen. A **Project** has at most one.
_Avoid_: Umbrella project, container

**Related Project**:
A **Project** linked to another as a peer, with neither part of the other,
for example Nereid and this website.
_Avoid_: See also, linked project

**Role**:
What Fabio did on a **Project**, such as author, contributor on build system
and CI, or PhD researcher.
_Avoid_: Position, contribution

**Status**:
Where a **Project** stands: Active, Finished, or Archived (stopped or no longer
maintained).
_Avoid_: State, phase

**Stack**:
The languages and tools a **Project** was built with, such as Python and
Blender.
_Avoid_: Tech, technologies, tools

**Featured Project**:
A **Project** shown on the home page.
_Avoid_: Highlight, pinned project

**Output**:
A finished artifact a **Project** produced, such as a thesis, paper, talk or
poster. A repository or live site is where a **Project** lives, not an
**Output**.
_Avoid_: Deliverable, result, publication

**Post**:
A dated piece of Fabio's writing published on the site. A **Post** can be
about one or more **Projects**. Anything published on the site is a **Post**,
never an **Output**.
_Avoid_: Article, blog post, essay, entry

**Series**:
A named sequence of **Posts** read in a set order, such as Building Nereid. A
**Series** can be about **Projects** but doesn't have to be, and a **Post**
belongs to at most one **Series**.
_Avoid_: Collection, thread, sequence

**Topic**:
One of a short, fixed list of themes Fabio returns to, such as AI-assisted
engineering. **Posts** and **Projects** can both have **Topics**.
_Avoid_: Tag, category, subject
